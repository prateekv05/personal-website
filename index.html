About

I'm a founder and product builder who likes turning messy problems into useful products.

I previously built Urban Rebox, a waste-tech company in Hyderabad, from the ground up. Over five years, I worked across product, operations, and growth, helping build a business that reached $200K in annual recurring revenue.

After completing my master's in Business Analytics at the University of Delaware, I started building a new generation of software products — taking ideas from concept to working applications and learning directly from the people who use them.

I'm currently building Tellibo, Introly, and Untangle in Cincinnati, Ohio.
