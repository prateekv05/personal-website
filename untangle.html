03 · DAILY LIFE

Untangle

A messy list in. A realistic day out.

Open the live app ↗

The story

What it is

You say everything on your mind, in any order. Untangle turns that into a day you can actually do, with a reason for each block, and re-plans when the day changes.

Why I built it

When everything feels urgent, planning the day can become another task. I wanted to build something that takes a messy brain dump and turns it into a realistic plan, without making the user organize everything first.

What I built

Tasks go in as plain language and come out as an organized daily schedule, with a reason behind each block. Instead of sorting through a long list by hand, users turn the chaos into actionable next steps and spend less time planning their day.

What I learned

I built the first version in a day. It reminded me how quickly I can go from an idea to a working product, but also that shipping is only the first step. Getting people to discover it, try it, and return is a different challenge from building it.

Where it stands

Built and deployed on Vercel. I've started sharing it publicly, and I'm exploring how to get it in front of the right users and learn whether it becomes part of their routine.
